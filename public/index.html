      Why plant in Kuwait?

      🌡️

      Kuwait regularly reaches 50°C in summer. Trees can cool streets by several degrees through shade and evaporation.

      🌪️

      Plants hold sandy soil in place and act as windbreaks, reducing dust storms.

      💨

      Every tree absorbs CO₂ and filters dust from the air we breathe.

      💧

      Native desert plants like Sidr, Ghaf and Arfaj need very little water once established.

      Smart watering tips

      • Water early in the morning or after sunset to avoid evaporation.
      • Water deeply but less often — it trains roots to grow down.
      • Cover soil with mulch or gravel to keep it cool and moist.
      • Autumn (Oct–Nov) and late winter (Feb–Mar) are the best planting seasons.
      • Reuse water from washing vegetables or your AC condensate.
